规则 Rules
  • 落子 Players take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection. Black plays first.
  • 提子 A group with no empty points next to it (no liberties) is captured and removed.
  • 禁着 You may not play a move that leaves your own group with no liberties, unless it captures.
  • 打劫 Ko: you may not immediately retake a single stone that just captured one of yours.
  • 终局 The game ends after both players pass in a row. Mark dead stones, then count.
  • 数子 Chinese area scoring: stones on the board plus surrounded empty points. White gets 7.5 komi (贴 3¾ 子).
